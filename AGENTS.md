# Skye Arc Dynamic Preview Rules
- Purpose: High-converting "Digital Dispatch" concept previews for US/AU construction contractors.
- Mandatory Safety:
  1. Hardcode `<meta name="robots" content="noindex, nofollow" />` in `app/layout.tsx`.
  2. Sticky top banner on every page: "CONCEPT PREVIEW ONLY — Built by skyeArc AI Technologies for [Company Name]. Not live or publicly indexed."
  3. NEVER scrape or use a prospect's copyrighted project photos or logo files. Use clean typographic wordmarks and vetted trade stand-in photos labeled "Sample project photo — replaced with your real portfolio on launch."
  4. NEVER invent testimonials, guarantees, or promise "more leads" or revenue.
- Localization: AU = "quotes" / casual direct tone. US = "estimates/quotes" / direct blue-collar tone.
- Required Embeds: GHL Quote Form iframe, GHL Booking Calendar iframe, and n8n "Skye" chat widget.
